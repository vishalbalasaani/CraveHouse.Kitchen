CRAVE HOUSE - IMAGES ASSETS DIRECTORY
================================================================
Please place your image assets inside this folder.

Recommended files:
1. logo.png (Your brand logo. Transparent background, high resolution. Change CRAVE_HOUSE_CONFIG.logoImageSrc in script.js to "assets/images/logo.png" to activate it)
2. hero-dessert.jpg (Main dessert bowl image on the hero section)
3. about-story.jpg (Visual about page story image)
4. menu/ (Create a subfolder here for menu item images)
5. reviews/ (Create a subfolder here for customer avatar images)

The website is currently configured to load premium, high-resolution royalty-free food images from Unsplash by default, so it looks complete immediately. Once you place your files here, simply update the paths in index.html and script.js!
